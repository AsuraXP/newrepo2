TITLE (alternates)

DESCRIPTION
You wake up ravenous. The light is wrong, the building is too quiet, and your phone says seven days
have passed since you came home from the stairwell.

A man lunged at you. You shoved him off. He caught your arm, and you told yourself it was a scrape.

The bite is gone now. The skin is smooth. And the hunger has hands.

Not a survival story. No chase, no rescue, no cure. You are not afraid of the bite - you are the
thing that walked through the week you cannot remember.

Written in second person. Headphones recommended.
