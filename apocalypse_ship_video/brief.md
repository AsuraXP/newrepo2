## Premise (v2 spine — AWAITING APPROVAL)

**Logline:** Dead-drunk and dead-tired after work, you brush off the
stranger who sinks his teeth into your arm — because he looked like just
another drunk. You wake up one week later to the end of the world. The bite
healed like a scratch. You're immune — and that's where the easy part ends:
the realization you're the only one is NOT the ending, it's the start.

**Beat spine v2:**

1. **COLD OPEN (0:00, H3):** wake-up → bathroom mirror, you poke the faded
   bite mark. Deadpan confession: a zombie bit you, you're still human —
   open loop: *why?* → "Let me rewind."
2. **The bite night (flashback):** normal street, ordinary-looking guy —
   "just a drunk" — teeth on your arm. You shrug it off. Home. Lights out.
3. **The wake-up (+1 week):** splitting headache, no memory of the week.
   Micro-tension: *where did the week go?*
4. **Window reveal (spectacle):** growling; swarms — no hands, half-eaten,
   bodies, gridlock. "Still a dream," you say.
5. **TV confirmation:** apocalypse broadcast, global disaster. Reality snap.
6. **The memory:** the bite returns — *he was one of them.* Open loop:
   *why haven't you turned?*
7. **THE DOOR TEST (midpoint set piece):** you step out; zombies right
   there — they don't come for you. One gets close. Does nothing.
8. **Immunity confirmed:** the bite did nothing; you're the only one in the
   world — *"and that's where the easy part ended."* → ACT 3 begins.
9. **ACT 3 — it's just the start (many things, ~5 min):**
   a. **Walking free:** you stroll through the horde untouched — dark
      comedy spectacle (looting mid-herd, crossing gridlock).
   b. **First contact with the living:** survivors see you unprotected
      among the dead — guns up, trust problem, almost shot.
   c. **Why me?** the mystery loop: was it the bite, or were you always
      different? (open loop for later payoff)
   d. **The word gets out:** cams/radio — factions converge: "his blood is
      the cure" vs. people who want this weapon kept for themselves.
   e. **THE TURN:** zombies don't just ignore you — they've started
      *following* you. Silent. Everywhere. They were never ignoring you —
      they were **waiting.**
10. **ENDING HOOK (stacked, cut to black):** most-wanted man alive + the
    horde trailing behind you + one last open-loop line → Part 2 fuel.

**Retention architecture:** open-loop chain (why shrug it off? → missing
week? → why not turned? → why me? → what are they waiting for?) ·
micro-tension every 15–20 s · spectacles at window reveal + door test +
horde walk · midpoint = door test · double cliffhanger ending.
