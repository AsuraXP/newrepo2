794 NAMES / your name isn't one

DESCRIPTION
At eleven every night, the tower on the east point reads out the names of the
living. It does not check whether you still are one.

Day 412. Third floor. One dog, one rifle with four rounds, and a radio that keeps
better hours than you do. Eight hundred and forty-one names last month. Seven
hundred and ninety-four tonight. You have listened for four hundred and twelve
nights and it has never said yours.

Four problems stand between you and being counted: the stairs, the water, the
noise, and the living. Here is the arithmetic on all four.

A fictional survival story, narrated. Animated.
