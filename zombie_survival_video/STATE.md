## What changed and why

Owner supplied a reference video (a supermarket siege, ~1,900 words, ~10 min) and
asked for that *type*. The old draft shared only POV with it. Diagnosis:

| | Reference | Draft 1/2 | Draft 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Structure | numbered problems + day markers | lyrical beats | numbered problems + day markers |
| Engine | arithmetic (9,000 cans ÷ 4/day = 6 years) | atmosphere | arithmetic (574 kg of paint, 200 L ÷ 1 L/day) |
| Refrain | "New store policy." | none | "House rule one. Nobody gets in." → amended |
| Voice | deadpan, "here is the thing nobody tells you" | literary simile | deadpan, one explicit "here is the thing" beat |
| Ending | ledger, then a knock | image of an open door | ledger, then a knock |

**Runtime tradeoff, stated plainly:** the reference is double our locked 300 s.
Draft 3 keeps the locked target at 293 s by running four problems tight instead
of the reference's ten or so sections. If the owner wants the reference's actual
density — the slow accumulation of days — this goes to ~1,700 words / ~9 min, and
that is a script-level change that must happen *now*, before any frames exist.
