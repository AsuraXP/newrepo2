Title: Why Did Ancient Humans Stop Sleeping Twice a Night?

Description:
If you wake up at 3:00 AM staring at the ceiling, feeling anxious that you can't fall back asleep—you might not be broken. For hundreds of thousands of years, humans slept in two shifts: the "first sleep" and the "second sleep," separated by an hour of peaceful, creative midnight wakefulness. Here is why we stopped, and how the modern world rewired your brain.
